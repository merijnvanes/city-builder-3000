# Experience target

Build toward the city-building experience of SimCity 3000 with original code, artwork and audio. A functioning prototype is not parity. Use its [manual](https://manualmachine.com/gamespc/simcity3000unlimited/1118664-user-manual/) as behavioral reference, alongside direct browser playtesting.

## Iteration 2 — city-building foundations (in progress)

- Classic compact tool groups, construction submenus, readable bottom information bar and news ticker.
- Rectangle zoning, three density settings, road/utility drag previews, upfront costs, cancel and undo.
- Separate R/C/I taxes, department funding, loans, ordinances, actual service coverage, garbage, health and education.
- Power-line and underground water-pipe tools; density and utility limits; positive and negative demand tied to jobs, housing, services and tax.
- Architectural variety and recognizable low/medium/high-density districts; ground textures, better shadows and street detail.
- Meaningful data maps, graphs and advisor messages; all controls connected to simulation.
- Save migration, deterministic simulation, balance scenarios, desktop/mobile browser checks.

## Following passes

- Wider transport choices and commuting pressure; neighborhood variation and redevelopment.
- Scenarios, objectives, disasters/emergency response and regional contracts.
- Terrain editing, more civic and special buildings, camera rotation, richer ambient city animation/audio.
- Compare the complete build against the reference and retain an honest list of remaining differences.

## Completion discipline

Finish, playtest, review and correct each integrated pass. Do not describe partial feature coverage as a match to the original game. Preserve saved cities when schema evolves. Keep ordinary computers and connections in scope.
